How to Buy LA28 Olympics Tickets in 2027

Where to buy official tickets, how to prepare for first-come sales and how to avoid scams.

September 30, 2026 · 9 min read · The LA City Guide Editorial Team

The next general-public opportunity to buy LA28 Olympic tickets is scheduled for 2027. This guide explains the official sales path, how to prepare before inventory opens, and how to avoid letting one hard-to-get session derail your Los Angeles trip.

Updated September 30, 2026: LA28 says its final general-public ticket drop of 2026 has ended. Olympic tickets are expected to return in 2027 on a first-come, first-served basis, while Paralympic tickets are expected to go on sale for the first time.

What Changes in 2027

The 2026 ticket drops used registrations, drawings and assigned purchase windows. According to the official LA28 ticketing page, the 2027 phase is planned as first-come, first-served purchasing. LA28 also says refreshed inventory across Olympic sports will be offered, subject to availability.

The practical implication is simple: speed will matter, but preparation will matter more. Decide which sports, dates, venue zones and price levels you will accept before sales open. A flexible shortlist gives you more ways to leave checkout with a workable trip.

Official-source rule: Start at LA28.org or Tickets.LA28.org. If a sales message, payment page or social post cannot be traced to the official site, pause before entering personal or payment information.

Before Tickets Go Live

  1. Create or verify your official profile. Use an email address you monitor and store the password securely.
  2. Subscribe to official updates. Sale dates and inventory details can change, so rely on LA28 announcements rather than old screenshots.
  3. Set a total ticket budget. Include fees and leave room for transportation, food and lodging.
  4. Build three session lists. Label them must-see, strong alternative and wildcard.
  5. Check every venue location. “Los Angeles” events extend across a large region, and some sports are outside LA County.
  6. Agree on group rules. Know who is purchasing, how much each person will spend and whether split seating is acceptable.

Build a Smarter Shortlist

Choose sessions as part of a travel plan, not as isolated tickets. Start with your most important sport, then look for another session in the same venue zone or on the same side of the region. This reduces cross-county travel and lowers the chance that traffic or a long transit connection causes a missed start.

What to Expect at Checkout

Inventory can move quickly. Keep the official page open, sign in early if LA28 instructs buyers to do so, and have an accepted payment method ready. Read the ticket category, session date, venue and quantity before paying. Do not assume that a sport name means a medal final; preliminary rounds and finals are different sessions.

Save the confirmation email after purchase, but do not post barcodes, order numbers or other sensitive details publicly. Seat assignments and mobile-ticket delivery timing may follow separate rules, so preserve every official email.

Avoid Ticket Scams

Be cautious with unsolicited direct messages, pressure to pay by wire transfer or cryptocurrency, screenshots offered as “proof,” and sellers who refuse to use an authorized marketplace. LA28 says its ticketing is powered by official providers AXS and Eventim; always confirm the current list on LA28.org before using any marketplace.

Verified resale is expected to open in 2027, but policies and approved platforms may change. Never treat a search-engine advertisement as proof that a seller is authorized.

After You Buy

Record each session’s date, start time, venue zone and ticket holder in one shared itinerary. Then choose lodging around the sessions that are hardest to reach, not simply around the attraction you know best. Our venue-zone hotel guide and LA transport guide can help.

Keep refundable lodging options where possible until your schedule settles. Do not book a same-day flight arrival for a high-priority event; airport delays, baggage and regional travel can consume more time than expected.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will Olympic tickets be available again?

LA28 says Olympic tickets are expected to return in 2027 with refreshed inventory, subject to availability.

When do Paralympic tickets go on sale?

LA28 currently says the first Paralympic ticket opportunity will be in 2027. Check the official ticketing page for the announced date and process.

Should I buy a hotel before tickets?

A refundable reservation can protect your options, but avoid locking into a nonrefundable property until you understand where your confirmed sessions are located.
