    Best Neighborhoods for First-Time LA Olympics Visitors

    Published: August 2026 · 7 min read · The LA City Guide Editorial Team

    Los Angeles spans nearly 500 square miles, and for a first-time visitor coming for the 2028 Olympics, that scale can be overwhelming. Picking the wrong base means longer Metro rides, pricier rideshares, and less time actually watching events.

    This guide breaks down the easiest neighborhoods for newcomers, matched to how you'll actually spend your days: watching events, eating well, and getting some rest in between.

    1. Santa Monica — Best for a Relaxed First Trip

    Ideal for: Visitors who want beach downtime between events and don't mind a longer Metro ride to inland venues.

    Santa Monica pairs walkable dining and the Pier with a direct Expo Line connection into Downtown, making it forgiving for visitors who haven't used LA transit before.

    • Crypto.com Arena: ~40-50 min by Metro
    • SoFi Stadium: ~35-45 min by Metro/shuttle combo
    • Best for: first 2-3 days of a trip, easing into the city

    2. Downtown LA — Best for Transit Access

    Ideal for: Visitors prioritizing minimal travel time between multiple venues.

    DTLA sits at the center of the Metro rail network. If your itinerary bounces between Crypto.com Arena, Long Beach, and Pasadena in the same week, Downtown cuts your average commute significantly compared to a coastal base.

    3. West Hollywood — Best for Food and Nightlife

    Ideal for: Visitors who want strong dining and nightlife without sacrificing central location.

    WeHo sits between Hollywood and Beverly Hills, an easy rideshare to most venues, with some of the city's best restaurant density within walking distance of most hotels.

    Sample First-Time 3-Day Plan

    Day 1: Arrive, settle into Santa Monica, easy beach walk. Day 2: Metro into Downtown for events at Crypto.com Arena. Day 3: Rideshare to Inglewood for SoFi Stadium, dinner in Downtown on the way back. For a fuller breakdown, see our 3-day LA Olympics itinerary.

    Is Santa Monica too far from most LA28 venues?

    Not for a short stay. It works best when your schedule has 1-2 events a day rather than back-to-back venues across the city.
