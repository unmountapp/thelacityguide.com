    Where to Stay in LA Based on Your Olympic Venue

    Published: August 2026 · 8 min read · The LA City Guide Editorial Team

    Most LA28 ticket holders don't pick a hotel first — they pick events first, then realize their venues are scattered across four distinct zones of the city. Matching your stay to those zones can save hours of travel over a multi-day trip.

    Here's how the main zones break down, and which base makes sense for each. For full venue details, see our LA28 venue zones explainer.

    SoFi Stadium / Inglewood Zone

    Best base: Inglewood, Culver City, or coastal Playa Vista/El Segundo.

    If most of your tickets are for opening ceremony or athletics at SoFi, staying south of Downtown cuts your commute dramatically compared to a Hollywood or Downtown hotel.

    Downtown / Crypto.com Arena Zone

    Best base: Downtown LA or Koreatown.

    This zone benefits the most from Metro rail access, since Crypto.com Arena sits at 7th/Metro Center, a hub connecting to nearly every other zone in this list.

    Pasadena / Rose Bowl Zone

    Best base: Pasadena, Old Town, or nearby Eagle Rock.

    The Rose Bowl sits apart from the rest of LA's Metro grid, so staying nearby avoids a lengthy return trip after evening events. See our full Rose Bowl match-day guide for logistics.

    UCLA / Westwood Zone

    Best base: Westwood, Santa Monica, or Brentwood.

    If your schedule centers on UCLA venues, a Westside stay keeps you close and still gives you beach access on rest days.

    Staying Near Your Venue vs. Staying Central

    Staying near a single venue minimizes daily travel but limits flexibility if you also have tickets elsewhere. A central Downtown base is the better compromise for visitors with a mixed, multi-zone schedule.
