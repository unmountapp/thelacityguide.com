Best Restaurants Near SoFi Stadium Before or After an Olympic Event

Last updated: June 2025  |  Food Guide  |  LA 2028 Olympics

SoFi Stadium sits in Inglewood, a city that has transformed remarkably in the last decade. What was once an underserved area now has a growing food scene, a walkable entertainment district, and good transport connections. If you're heading to an Olympic event at SoFi, there are solid options to eat well both before and after — without fighting for an Uber to a restaurant in another part of LA.

Practical note: On major event days (especially the Opening Ceremony), restaurants near SoFi will be busy. Arrive early or book in advance for sit-down meals. Street food and casual options are your best bet for post-event eating when wait times at restaurants spike.

The Hollywood Park Entertainment District (Closest to SoFi)

SoFi Stadium sits within the Hollywood Park development, which includes a retail and dining district designed to serve the stadium crowds. This is the most convenient option if you want to eat within easy walking distance of the venue.

Best for: Pre-game atmosphere, groups

The Truss (at Hollywood Park)

~5 min walk to stadium $$ American / Bar food

The Truss is the main bar and restaurant concept anchoring the Hollywood Park outdoor plaza. It's built for high volume — think craft burgers, wings, and cold beer served efficiently. Expect crowds and a buzzing pre-event atmosphere. Good for groups who want a simple meal before the event without going far.

Best for: Quick grab before the event

Hollywood Park Food Hall

~5 min walk $–$$ Multiple cuisines

The Hollywood Park development includes food hall-style vendors serving a range of cuisines from tacos to sushi rolls. Fast, reasonably priced, and flexible for groups with mixed tastes. Ideal when you want something quick without committing to a full sit-down experience.

Inglewood City Area (10–15 min walk or 5 min rideshare)

Inglewood's city centre just north of the stadium has independent restaurants and local spots that have operated for years. These tend to be more affordable and more local in character than the Hollywood Park options.

Best for: Local soul food experience

Harold & Belle's

~10 min walk $$ Creole / Soul food

Harold & Belle's is an Inglewood institution — a family-run Creole and soul food restaurant that's been open since the late 1960s. Shrimp po'boys, fried catfish, and gumbo are the kinds of dishes you'll find here. This is the real Inglewood food culture. Book ahead for weekend events.

Best for: Pre-event tacos on a budget

Las Carnitas Tacos

~10–12 min walk $ Mexican / Tacos

A no-frills taqueria serving excellent carnitas, carne asada, and birria tacos. One of several affordable Mexican spots in the Inglewood area. Quick service makes it practical before an event. Cash-friendly.

Best for: Halal options

Local halal restaurants on Manchester Ave

~10–15 min walk or 5 min rideshare $–$$ Halal / Various

Manchester Avenue running through Inglewood has several halal restaurants serving African, Middle Eastern, and fusion cuisine. Good for Muslim visitors or anyone looking for halal-certified food near the venue. Options vary — a quick Google Maps search for "halal near SoFi Stadium" will show current open options.

Eating Inside SoFi Stadium

SoFi Stadium has an extensive array of in-stadium food and beverage vendors, including local restaurant partnerships. Previous Rams and Chargers events have featured options ranging from tacos to sushi to BBQ within the venue. Prices are stadium-standard (expect to pay a premium), but the variety is genuinely good by stadium standards.

For the 2028 Olympics, the in-venue food program will be managed by LA28 and the stadium operator. Check the official stadium app or la28.org closer to your event for the confirmed vendor list.

If You Have More Time: Culver City or Hawthorne

If you're arriving well before an event or heading out early after, the areas of Culver City and Hawthorne (both 10–20 minutes by rideshare) have a much broader and more developed restaurant scene. This is worth considering for special pre-event dinners rather than a quick bite.

  • Culver City: A dense strip of excellent restaurants on Washington Blvd. See Metro E Line access from this area.
  • Hawthorne: Growing food scene with a mix of casual and quality spots. Less tourist traffic than Culver City.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there good food near SoFi Stadium?

Yes — the Hollywood Park development adjacent to SoFi has grown significantly and now includes a variety of restaurants and food halls. Inglewood city centre also has long-standing local spots worth visiting.

Can I eat inside SoFi Stadium during Olympic events?

Yes. SoFi has extensive in-venue food and beverage options. Pricing is higher than outside the stadium. The specific vendors for LA28 events haven't been confirmed as of mid-2025 — check la28.org closer to your event.

How far in advance should I book a restaurant before an event?

For popular event days (Opening Ceremony, major athletics sessions), book 2–4 weeks in advance for sit-down restaurants. For casual spots and food halls, no reservation needed — just arrive early.

Are there vegan or vegetarian options near SoFi?

LA has an excellent vegan and vegetarian food scene generally. Options near SoFi are growing but more limited than in areas like West Hollywood or Silver Lake. The Hollywood Park food hall typically includes plant-based options. For more choice, Culver City (10 minutes away) has numerous vegan-friendly restaurants.

Disclosure: Restaurant information was compiled from publicly available sources as of mid-2025. Hours, menus, and availability may change. Always verify current status before your visit.
