LA 2028 Olympic Venues Guide

The 2028 Los Angeles Olympics will be hosted across several world-class venues spread throughout the greater LA area. Each venue has its own character, transport options, and nearby accommodation. Use these guides to plan your Olympic experience.

SoFi Stadium, Inglewood

The centerpiece of LA28. SoFi Stadium will host athletics, the opening ceremony, and closing ceremony. Located in Inglewood next to the Intuit Dome.

Crypto.com Arena, Downtown LA

Home to LA Lakers and Clippers, this iconic downtown arena will host basketball and boxing events during the 2028 Games.

Rose Bowl Stadium, Pasadena

The historic Rose Bowl in Pasadena will host football (soccer) events. One of the most iconic stadium venues in American sports.

Dignity Health Sports Park, Carson

This multi-use stadium in Carson will host rugby and other field sports during the LA28 Olympics.
