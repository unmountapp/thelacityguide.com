🏟️ Olympic Venue Guide

Your complete guide to every LA 2028 Olympic venue — with transport tips, nearby food, and what to expect.

🏈 Athletics / Opening

SoFi Stadium

Home of Super Bowls and now the Olympics Opening Ceremony. Inglewood's crown jewel seats 70,000+ fans.

🚇 K Line to Inglewood Transit Center, then free shuttle
🏀 Basketball Finals

Crypto.com Arena

The Staples Center reborn. Home of the Lakers and Clippers, now hosting Olympic basketball finals downtown.

🚇 Metro Red/Purple Line to 7th/Metro Center
🏊 Swimming / Diving

Uytengsu Aquatics Center (USC)

The University of Southern California's state-of-the-art aquatics facility, rebuilt for Olympic glory.

🚌 Metro Expo Line to Exposition Park stop
🏋️ Gymnastics

Pauley Pavilion (UCLA)

UCLA's legendary arena, home to 11 NCAA championships, now hosts the world's best gymnasts.

🚌 Rapid Bus 2 from Santa Monica or Westwood
⚽ Football / Soccer

Rose Bowl Stadium

Pasadena's iconic venue hosts soccer, set against the backdrop of the San Gabriel Mountains.

🚌 Shuttle from Pasadena Metro Gold Line stations
🏐 Beach Volleyball

Long Beach Arena

Beach volleyball by the Pacific — sun, sand, and the world's best players in the ultimate setting.

🚇 Metro Blue Line to Downtown Long Beach
🎾 Tennis

LA Tennis Center (UCLA)

UCLA's world-class tennis facility features 22 courts including the legendary Stade Roland Garros-style center court.

🚌 Rapid Bus 2 via Wilshire Blvd
🚴 Cycling / Track

StubHub Center Velodrome

The only Olympic velodrome in LA, rebuilt to UCI standards for track cycling events.

🚌 Metro Silver Line from downtown LA
🤸 Wrestling / Judo

Los Angeles Convention Center

The massive convention center transforms into a world-class combat sports arena in the heart of downtown.

🚇 Metro Blue/Expo Line to Pico Station

    🚇 Getting Around LA

    LA gets a bad rap for traffic. Here's how locals actually get around during the Games.

    🚇 Metro Rail

    LA's expanding Metro connects key Olympic venues. B/D Lines serve UCLA and downtown. K Line serves Inglewood and SoFi Stadium. Free Olympic shuttles augment coverage.

    🚌 Olympic Shuttle Network

    Free point-to-point shuttles run between all 19 Olympic venues during Games time. Download the LA28 app for real-time schedules and route planning.

    🚲 Metro Bike Share

    Over 1,500 bikes and 170 stations across LA. Perfect for Venice Beach to Santa Monica runs. Monthly passes available — far cheaper than parking.

    🛴 Bird & Lime Scooters

    Electric scooters dominate the beachside path from Malibu to Venice to Long Beach. Helmet use is mandatory and thankfully increasingly common.

    🚗 Rideshare

    Uber and Lyft surge significantly during Games. Book 30 minutes early for key events. Designated pickup zones operate at every venue — follow signage carefully.

    🚆 Metrolink & Amtrak

    Regional rail connects LA to Anaheim (30 min), San Diego (3hr), and Santa Barbara (2.5hr). Book early — trains sell out during peak Olympics weeks.

    🚁 Uber Copter / Blade

    Premium helicopter shuttles operate LAX-to-downtown and LAX-to-Malibu during peak periods. Expensive but spectacular — book via respective apps.

    ✈️ LAX Airport Tips

    LAX Automated People Mover opens in 2028 connecting to Metro. Budget 2.5 hours for international arrivals. Terminal 7/8 and TBIT are the largest international hubs.

    🚗 Day Trips from LA

    Use a free day to explore Southern California. These trips are all doable before your evening event.

    🏰 Disneyland Resort, Anaheim

    The Happiest Place on Earth is just 40 minutes away. Both Disneyland and Disney California Adventure in one day. Buy tickets months in advance for summer 2028.

    45 min · I-5 South or Metrolink Orange County Line

    🌊 Malibu & Pacific Coast Highway

    Drive PCH north and stop at Zuma Beach, Point Dume, and El Matador State Beach. Lunch at Nobu Malibu. Return via Mulholland Drive at sunset.

    35 min · PCH / Route 1

    🍷 Santa Barbara Wine Country

    Santa Ynez Valley, Solvang (Danish village), and 40+ wineries. Sideways wine country scenery less than 2 hours north on US-101.

    1.5-2 hrs · US-101 North

    🏜️ Joshua Tree National Park

    Surreal desert landscapes, rock climbing, and the most spectacular stargazing in Southern California. Combine with Palm Springs for a perfect overnight.

    2.5 hrs · I-10 East to CA-62

    🐬 San Diego

    Balboa Park, the Zoo, Little Italy, La Jolla Cove. World-class beaches just 2.5 hours south. Also hosting some Olympic sailing events.

    2-3 hrs · I-5 South or Amtrak Pacific Surfliner

    🌲 Big Bear Lake

    Mountain escape just 2 hours away. Hiking, paddleboarding, and alpine air. Famous for Olympic-level cycling training routes used by athletes themselves.

    2 hrs · I-10 East to CA-18

    🌍 International Visitor Guides

    Everything international visitors need to know — from visas to currency to cultural tips.

    🇬🇧 UK Visitors

    No visa required (ESTA needed). British pubs in Santa Monica. Drive on the right. Tipping 18-20% is standard. LA is 8 hours behind London.

    🇯🇵 Japanese Visitors

    Japanese-speaking staff at key hotels. Little Tokyo in DTLA for familiar food. Punctuality differs here — rideshare apps recommended. No visa (90 days).

    🇦🇺 Australian Visitors

    No visa needed (ESTA). Bondi expats everywhere in Venice Beach. Coffee culture thriving. Uber Eats delivers everything. Time zone: LA is 17-18 hrs behind Sydney.

    🇧🇷 Brazilian Visitors

    Visa required — apply 3+ months early. Brazilian community in Westwood. Portuguese-speaking services available. Soccer events will have strong Brazilian fan presence.

    🇨🇳 Chinese Visitors

    Visa required. Chinatown in DTLA and San Gabriel Valley offer Chinese restaurants, supermarkets, and Mandarin/Cantonese speaking staff. WeChat Pay accepted at select venues.

    🇩🇪 German Visitors

    No visa (ESTA). German speakers common in tech/film industry. LA has no biergartens but Culver City beer scene is excellent. Time zone: 9 hours behind Berlin.

    🇫🇷 French Visitors

    No visa (ESTA). French restaurants in West Hollywood and Beverly Hills. LA has a large French expat community. Avoid using AZERTY keyboards — US standard.

    🇲🇽 Mexican Visitors

    Visa required. LA's largest cultural community with 4M+ Mexican-Americans. Spanish is widely spoken everywhere. East LA and Boyle Heights are home.

    🛂 Practical Info for All International Visitors

    Currency: USD. Major cards accepted everywhere. ATMs at all venues.
    Power: 110V/60Hz. Type A/B plugs. Bring a universal adapter.
    Emergency: Dial 911 for police, fire, and medical.
    SIM Cards: T-Mobile and AT&T offer tourist eSIM plans from $30/month.
    Health: Travel insurance strongly recommended. Hospitals near all venues.
    Tipping: 18-20% at restaurants, $1-2/drink at bars, $5/night hotel housekeeping.
