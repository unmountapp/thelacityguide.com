Getting Around

Navigate LA Like a Pro

Yes, you can get around LA without a car. Here's everything you need to know.

🚇

Metro Rail

LA's growing rail network connects DTLA to Santa Monica (E Line), Long Beach (A Line), Pasadena (L Line), and more Olympic venues.

💡 Get a TAP card ($2 for card, $1.75/ride). Unlimited day pass is $7.
🚌

Metro Bus

700+ bus routes cover every corner of LA. Rapid lines skip stops for faster service. Same TAP card as rail.

💡 Use the Metro app for real-time arrivals. Google Maps Metro integration works great too.
🚲

Metro Bike

Bikeshare stations near most Metro stops. Electric bikes available. Great for beach routes, Arts District, and Griffith Park areas.

💡 First 30 min free with TAP card. Electric bikes are $1.70/30 min after free period.
🚗

Rideshare (Uber/Lyft)

Essential for neighborhoods not on Metro. Surge pricing during events — download both apps and compare. Budget $15-25 for most cross-city trips.

💡 Order rideshare to nearby pick-up points, not right at venues. Beat the post-event surge by leaving 20 min before end.
🚆

Metrolink

Regional rail connects LA to Pasadena, Long Beach, Riverside (for Lake Perris), and beyond. Essential for day trips and outer venues.

💡 Olympic Games Metrolink passes will be available — great value for multi-day visitors.
🦶

Walk + Transit Combos

Santa Monica, Venice, DTLA, and Koreatown are all very walkable. Walk to transit, not to your final destination in LA.

💡 AllTrails for walking trails, Google Maps for transit. Download offline maps before big event days.

International Visitors

Welcome to LA — from Everywhere

We know you're coming from all over the world. Here's what you need to know about visiting LA.

🇬🇧
United Kingdom
No visa required (ESTA). 10 hr flight from London. Use dollars. Drive on the right. LA weather is much better than home — pack sunscreen.
🇯🇵
Japan
No visa required (ESTA). 11 hr flight from Tokyo. Little Tokyo in DTLA will feel like home. LA has one of the largest Japanese-American communities in the world.
🇩🇪
Germany
No visa required (ESTA). 11 hr flight from Frankfurt. Strong German community in LA. LA is on the same longitude as Berlin in some ways — lots of arts and culture.
🇧🇷
Brazil
US tourist visa required. 12-14 hr flight from São Paulo. Huge Brazilian community in LA. Portuguese speakers will find their people here easily.
🇨🇳
China
US tourist visa required. Direct flights from major Chinese cities. San Gabriel Valley (SGV) has the best Chinese food outside China — don't miss it.
🇳🇬
Nigeria
US tourist visa required. 22+ hr journey. Growing Nigerian community in LA. Leimert Park and Inglewood have fantastic African dining and culture.
🇮🇳
India
US tourist visa required. Artesia ("Little India") is 30 min from DTLA. Incredible Indian food corridor. Cricket fans: yes, there will be cricket at LA28.
🇦🇺
Australia
No visa required (ESTA). 15 hr flight from Sydney. You'll feel at home — beaches, outdoor culture, and people who understand your humor.

🌍 A Message to All International Visitors

Despite recent headlines about US travel, Los Angeles is one of the most welcoming, diverse cities in the world. Over 140 languages are spoken here. You will find your community, your food, and your culture in LA. We're thrilled to host the world in 2028. You are welcome here.
