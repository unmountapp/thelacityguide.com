LA Metro Rail Guide

The complete guide to using LA Metro rail for the 2028 Olympics. Covers all lines, fares, key stations, and routes to each Olympic venue.

⚠ Fares, routes, and schedules are subject to change. Verify at metro.net and official sources. Last updated: June 2026.

Metro Rail Overview

LA Metro operates a growing rail network that connects many of the 2028 Olympic venues. It is not comprehensive — some venues require buses or rideshare connections — but for the major event corridors, Metro is the most reliable and affordable option, especially on high-demand event days when traffic and rideshare surge pricing are significant factors.

Fares

LA Metro fares as of 2026: $1.75 per ride. Day passes and multi-day passes available. TAP card required — purchase at station vending machines ($2 card fee + load fare). Contactless payment also accepted on most Metro equipment.

  • Single ride: $1.75
  • Day pass: $5.00
  • 7-day pass: $25.00 (verify current price at metro.net)
  • Monthly pass: $100.00 (not practical for visitors)
  • TAP card fee: $2.00 (reusable)

During the Olympics, Metro may offer special event passes or pricing. Monitor metro.net for announcements.

Venue Routes

SoFi Stadium (Opening/Closing Ceremonies, Athletics)

Metro K Line (Crenshaw) to planned Hollywood Park station. Extension status: verify at metro.net. Alternative: A Line to Rosa Parks station, transfer to K Line, then shuttle.

Crypto.com Arena (Basketball, Boxing)

Metro A or E Line to 7th/Metro Center — 5 min walk. Most direct venue from Downtown hotels.

Rose Bowl (Football/Soccer)

Metro A Line (Gold) to Memorial Park in Pasadena, then event day shuttle (~$3–5). From 7th/Metro Center: ~40 min.

Long Beach Arena (Weightlifting, Wrestling, Judo)

Metro A Line (Blue) to Downtown Long Beach station — 10 min walk. From 7th/Metro Center: ~60 min.

UCLA Campus (Gymnastics, Swimming, Tennis)

Metro D Line to Westwood/UCLA (verify 2026 extension status). Otherwise: E Line to Expo/Bundy + Big Blue Bus to campus.

Honda Center, Anaheim (Ice Hockey, Figure Skating)

Metrolink (separate from Metro) Orange County Line from Union Station to Anaheim-Canyon/ARTIC: ~50 min.

Santa Anita Park (Equestrian)

Metro A Line (Gold) to Arcadia station — 10–15 min walk. From Union Station: ~40 min.
