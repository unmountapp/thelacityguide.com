Koreatown

Koreatown is the most densely populated neighborhood in LA and one of the best-value bases for the 2028 Olympics. Great Korean BBQ, 24-hour dining, and Metro access to most venues.

About Koreatown

Koreatown (K-Town) sits between Hollywood and Downtown LA — a 10-minute Metro ride to either. It is dense, walkable by LA standards, and packed with Korean restaurants, bars, karaoke spots, and bakeries — many open past midnight. It is also the most cost-competitive neighborhood in central LA for hotels.

Koreatown is an ideal base: central location, excellent food at all price points, easy Metro connections, and enough late-night options to keep you busy after evening Olympic events.

Why Stay Here

  • Most hotels cost less than equivalent rooms in Hollywood or Santa Monica
  • Metro B Line and D Line (Red/Purple) at Wilshire/Vermont and Wilshire/Normandie stations
  • 15 min to DTLA (Crypto.com Arena) by Metro
  • Dense dining and bar scene, walkable at night
  • 24-hour restaurants — useful after late evening events

Where to Eat in Koreatown

Korean BBQ

  • Park's BBQ — consistently ranked the best Korean BBQ in LA; expect a wait
  • Quarters Korean BBQ — excellent cuts, strong banchan selection
  • Soowon Galbi — old-school K-Town institution, more casual

Beyond BBQ

  • Escuela Taqueria — Mexican-Korean fusion, late hours
  • Hangari Bajirak Kalguksu — knife-cut noodle soup, Wilshire
  • Dan Sung Sa — Korean pojangmacha (street food tavern), open late
  • Kbbq + Nightlife district around 6th St — restaurants, karaoke bars

Getting Around

Metro B Line (Red) and D Line (Purple) both serve the Wilshire/Vermont and Wilshire/Normandie stations. From Koreatown: 15 min to Downtown, 25 min to Hollywood, 40 min to Universal City. Koreatown is also a reasonable Uber/Lyft hub for reaching SoFi Stadium or Inglewood.

Where to Stay

  • Line Hotel Koreatown — design hotel with rooftop pool
  • Hannam Chain Hotel
  • Radisson Hotel Wilshire
  • Multiple mid-range options on Wilshire Blvd

Metro Access

  • B/D Line at Wilshire/Vermont or Wilshire/Normandie
  • To DTLA: ~15 min
  • To Hollywood: ~25 min
  • To SoFi via A+K Line: ~55 min
