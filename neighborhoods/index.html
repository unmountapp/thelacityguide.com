Best Neighborhoods to Stay for the LA 2028 Olympics

Los Angeles is a city of distinct neighborhoods, each with its own character, vibe, and proximity to Olympic venues. Whether you want to be steps from SoFi Stadium, walking distance from the beach, or immersed in cultural LA, we have a guide for you.

Downtown LA

The city urban core, home to Crypto.com Arena and close to major transit hubs. Great for fans attending indoor events.

Hollywood

Iconic and central, Hollywood puts you near the Walk of Fame, great dining, and easy freeway access to all Olympic zones.

Inglewood

Ground zero for the 2028 Games. SoFi Stadium and Intuit Dome are both here. Ideal for track and field and opening ceremonies.

Koreatown

Central, affordable, and full of incredible food. Koreatown is one of the best-value bases with strong Metro access.

Long Beach

Relaxed beach-city vibes with Olympic sailing and rowing events. A calmer alternative to staying in LA proper.

Pasadena

Home of the Rose Bowl and a charming Old Town. Perfect for visitors who want a quieter stay with easy venue access.

Venice and Santa Monica

Beachfront living with the iconic boardwalk and Santa Monica Pier. A top pick for visitors who want sun, sand, and sports.
