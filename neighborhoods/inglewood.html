Inglewood

Inglewood is home to SoFi Stadium — where the Opening and Closing Ceremonies and Athletics take place. The neighborhood is undergoing rapid development around Hollywood Park, though hotel options near the venue remain limited.

About Inglewood

Inglewood is a city south of LA proper, rapidly transforming around the Hollywood Park development that includes SoFi Stadium, the Kia Forum (concert venue), a town square, park, and hotels. The city has a rich African-American cultural history and a growing food scene.

For the 2028 Olympics, Inglewood will be the most intense venue zone — the Opening Ceremony on July 14 and Closing Ceremony both happen at SoFi Stadium, drawing capacity crowds from around the world.

Hollywood Park Development

  • SoFi Stadium — 70,240 seats, Opening/Closing Ceremonies + Athletics
  • Kia Forum — converted concert arena adjacent to SoFi
  • Hollywood Park Town Square — restaurants and shops
  • New hotels planned/under development (verify current status)

Getting There

The Metro K Line (Crenshaw/LAX Line) runs through Inglewood. An extension to a Hollywood Park station near SoFi Stadium is planned for 2028 — verify current status at metro.net. From LAX: SoFi Stadium is only 3 miles away — rideshare or taxi is practical (~10–20 min, $15–25).

Where to Eat in Inglewood

  • Hilltop Coffee + Kitchen — local staple, breakfast and brunch
  • The Serving Spoon — legendary Inglewood soul food, Market St
  • Randy's Donuts — 70+ year old Inglewood icon, open late
  • Hollywood Park Town Square — food options adjacent to SoFi
  • Truxton's American Bistro — local gastropub

Olympic Venue

  • SoFi Stadium — Opening/Closing Ceremonies, Athletics

Where to Stay

  • Hotel options near SoFi are limited — hotels near LAX (3 miles) are the practical choice
  • Book LAX corridor hotels well in advance

Getting There

  • From LAX: 3 miles (~$15–25 rideshare)
  • Metro K Line to Hollywood Park station (verify 2028 extension)
