About The LA City Guide

The LA City Guide is an independent travel and planning resource for visitors coming to Los Angeles for the 2028 Olympic and Paralympic Games. We publish practical, honestly-written guides to help international and domestic visitors navigate neighborhoods, venues, transport, dining, and trip logistics.

We are independently operated and not affiliated with LA28, the IOC, or the City of Los Angeles.

What we cover

  • Los Angeles neighborhoods — comparisons, character, proximity to venues
  • Olympic and Paralympic venues — all major LA28 sites with transport and dining context
  • Transport and airports — LAX, Metro, rideshare, and getting around without a car
  • International visitor guides — country-specific guides for UK, Japan, Brazil, Australia, France, Germany, Mexico, Canada, Nigeria, and more
  • Itinerary planning — 3-day guides, family-friendly routes, budget planning
  • Dining near venues — pre- and post-event restaurants for each major venue area

Audience

Our readers are planning trips to Los Angeles for the 2028 Games. They are in the research and planning phase, which means they are receptive to hotel, transport, tour, dining, and experience recommendations.
