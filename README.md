## 🏆 About

The LA City Guide is a comprehensive visitor companion built for the LA 2028 Olympics and beyond. With 1.5M+ international visitors expected during the Olympic window and $18B in projected economic impact, we're building the definitive local-written guide to Los Angeles.

## ✨ Features

- **Neighborhood Deep Dives** — Koreatown, Leimert Park, Arts District, Echo Park, Boyle Heights, and more
- **Olympic Venue Guide** — All 13 LA28 venues with transit, parking, and nearby dining
- **AI Itinerary Builder** — Personalized day-by-day LA plans based on your dates and interests
- **Eat Like a Local** — Real restaurant picks: Roscoe's, Bestia, Buka Nigerian, Grand Central Market
- **Getting Around** — Metro, bikeshare, rideshare, and Metrolink guides
- **Regional Day Trips** — San Diego, Santa Barbara, Palm Springs, Joshua Tree
- **International Visitor Info** — Welcome guides for visitors from 8+ countries
- **Newsletter** — Weekly LA insider updates
